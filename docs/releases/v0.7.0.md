### New: Empty your characters' personal stashes too
**Sort & Distribute** has a new picker, *Also empty the personal stash of*. Tick the characters you play, and their personal stash goes onto your mules along with the shared stash, sorted by category like everything else.
- Only the stash grid is emptied. Their inventory, cube and gear stay where they are, and so do the Horadric Cube itself and quest items.
- Items you've flagged ⚑ and categories under *Keep these in the stash* stay put too.
- Works with *Empty the shared stash*, *Tidy up* and *Full re-sort*. Your ticks are remembered.
- Resurrected-era characters can't use the RotW stash, so theirs is left alone and the preview says so.

In the terminal: `StashSorter.exe plan --from-chars "Shazam,Eorlingas"`.

### New: Rename any character
Click a character on the **Overview** and press **Rename…**. The save and its side files (key bindings, map) are renamed together. As with every change, your saves are backed up first and **Backups → Undo** puts the old name back. The name has to follow the game's rules (2–15 letters, at most one `-` or `_`) and mustn't be taken already.

In the terminal: `StashSorter.exe rename OldName NewName`.

### Better: See where items are in Find
Each result now shows where the item is without hovering, e.g. **CharmMule** · stash, or **Shared stash (RotW)** · tab 2. A small map of the grid lights up the exact cells it sits in.

**New to Stash Sorter?** Download `StashSorter.exe`, put it in its own folder, close the game completely and double-click it. Windows may warn about an unrecognised app (the exe isn't code-signed): *More info → Run anyway*. You can check the download against `StashSorter.exe.sha256`. Offline saves only.
