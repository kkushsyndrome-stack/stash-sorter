### Fixed: ⚑ "keep in stash" flags could stop working
A flagged item could lose its flag after a sort, a test run, or moving things around in the game, and the next sort would then move the item you meant to keep. Flags now stay on the right item, however the rest of the stash changes. (If you move the flagged item yourself in game, its flag comes off.)

Flags you set in 0.7.0 or earlier carry over automatically the first time you open each stash. It's worth a quick look at your ⚑ items once, in case one had already slipped onto the wrong item before you updated.

The ticks on **Clean Up → Duplicates** were affected in the same way and are fixed too.

### Changed: one Role per character
The **Overview** now gives each character a role instead of a tick box:
- **Mule**: receives sorted loot (same defaults as before).
- **Empty stash**: a character you play whose personal stash goes onto the mules when you sort.
- **Leave alone**: never touched.

This replaces the separate "Also empty the personal stash of" picker on the Sort & Distribute tab, which now just shows who will be emptied. Your current choices carry over.

### Also
- **Back up now** button on the Backups tab (it was only on the Terror Zones tab before).
- The Sort & Distribute modes explain the difference better: *Empty the shared stash* is the quickest, and *Tidy up* does all of that and more.
- Find shows shared-stash locations as e.g. "Shared stash (RotW) - tab 1".
